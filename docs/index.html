What it sends

TX sends these when you press TX or Tune. Opening the radio does not key.

📡 FT8, FT4, FT2

15 s, 7.5 s, and 3.75 s. DIGU. The MSHV generator. Contest lines Tx1–Tx6 are messages, not extra modes.

🖋️ Hell

Feld, Slow, X5, X9, FSK, FSK-105, and Hell 80, each at its Fldigi column rate. Feld Hell is 17.5 columns/s, 14 pixels, a 2 ms raised cosine. Hell notes.

⌨️ RTTY

One menu row. 45.45 baud, 170 Hz, mark high, 5 bits, 1.5 stop. A space sends LTRS, then the space.

〰️ BPSK

PSK31, 63, 125, 250, 500, and 1000. A zero reverses the phase. QPSK is not in this build.

🎼 Olivia and Contestia

4/250, 8/250, 8/500, 16/500, 16/1000, and 32/1000. Gray-coded tones.

🎚️ MFSK, DominoEX, Thor

MFSK4 through 128, NASA K=7. DominoEX 4–22 with FEC off. Thor 4–22, K=7 only. The long and micro rows stay off the menu.

📝 Macros

Supports importing Fldigi custom macros. Macro notes.

Receive only

🗞️ WEFAX-576

IOC 576, 120 lines per minute. Black is 400 Hz below the cursor and white is 400 Hz above it. It does not key, and it does not send START.

Still spreading

Not on the Mode menu. They do not key. The outbreak is incomplete on purpose.

On the bench

🌊 MT63

500, 1000, and 2000 Hz, short and long. The Walsh code checks out. A character has not yet come back out of the audio, so the menu stays clear of it.
